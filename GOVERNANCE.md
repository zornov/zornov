# Governance

This document says who makes decisions about Zornov and how.

## Steward

Zornov is a project of **Ashrox LLC** ("the steward"). The steward owns the
project's repositories, release and signing keys, domains, accounts and the
Zornov name and logos, and is responsible for the project's legal and
financial obligations.

The steward has the final say on:

- **Releases**: what is released, when, and under which version number; and
  which builds are signed with the project's keys and offered as updates.
- **Licensing**: the licence the project is distributed under, the terms on
  which contributions are accepted (see `CONTRIBUTING.md`), and the
  acceptance of third-party code, models and content and their licences.
- **Trademarks**: the use of the Zornov name and logos (see below).
- **Security**: handling vulnerability reports and advisories (see
  `SECURITY.md`).
- **Membership**: who is a maintainer, and the code of conduct and its
  enforcement.

## Maintainers

Maintainers review and merge changes, triage issues and look after parts of
the code. The steward appoints them, usually from contributors with a record
of good work and good judgement, and may remove them. Maintainers act on the
steward's behalf and within this document.

## How decisions are made

Most decisions are made in the open, in issues and pull requests, by rough
consensus among the maintainers who take part. A change is merged when a
maintainer approves it and no maintainer has an unresolved objection.

When consensus cannot be reached, or a decision falls in one of the areas
above, the steward decides. The steward explains significant decisions
publicly unless they involve security, legal or personal matters.

## Contributions

Contributions are welcome from anyone, on the terms in `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
Contributing does not by itself make someone a maintainer or give them a say
over the project's direction, releases, licence or name.

## Trademarks

Zornov™ and the Zornov logos are trademarks of Ashrox LLC, used to identify
its official releases and services. The source code licence does not grant
rights to them.

You may use the name to refer to the project truthfully, for example "a
plugin for Zornov" or "based on Zornov". Please do not use the name or logos
in a way that suggests your build, fork, product or service is official or
endorsed by Ashrox LLC. A modified build distributed to others should carry
a different name. Ask the steward if you are unsure.

Names of other products mentioned in this repository belong to their owners
and are used only to describe compatibility.

## Changes to this document

The steward may change this document. Changes are made by pull request so
that they are visible.
