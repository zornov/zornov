# Tool reference (long forms)

The full text each tool description used to carry. `guide {topic: "tool:<name>"}` returns one section.

## `layout`

The editor window's panels, docked as the person docks them: a tree of splits and tab stacks. action get (default): every panel (open, showing, floating), the tree and the workspaces. open {panel} / close {panel}; dock {panel, target: a panel or window, zone: left | right | top | bottom | tab}; float {panel} (review, record, editorial) into a window inside the editor; pop_out {panel} into a window of the system's own (any panel but the viewer), dock_back {panel} puts it where it was; workspace {name} switches (Edit, Colour, Audio, VFX, Motion graphics, Production or the person's own); save_workspace {name} keeps the layout as it is; delete_workspace {name}; reset back to Zornov's layout; set {tree} lays out a whole tree from get. Panels: library, viewer, inspector, timeline, sound (the mixer), scopes, review, record, editorial, ai (you: the AI editor). Only in the Zornov window; change the layout when the person asks, or when a panel they need for what you are doing together is closed.

## `taste`

What the person likes, remembered across requests and projects: their likes, dislikes, rules, the corrections they made and the work they rejected, and this project's style guide. No arguments reads it all (it also comes with each request, short). Save a lasting preference with remember (kind: like, dislike or rule; everywhere: true for all their projects); forget a note by id; write the project's style guide with style_guide (append: true to add to it).

## `edit`

Change the project with a list of ops applied as ONE undo step (all or nothing). Each op is {"op": name, ...fields}. Clips by id, "$1"/"$last" (made earlier in this call), "@4.2" / "music@4.2" (at a time), a name, or {lane, from, to}. Ops: scene, music, narrate, place, add, text, card, fx_layer | split, trim, delete, move, reorder, roll, slide, slip, duplicate, freeze, replace, detach_audio, link, unlink, group, ungroup | set (any property inspect lists), animate, keys, modifier (noise, loop, steps, limits, formula on a property), physics (layers fall, bounce and stack, baked to keys), driver (a property follows other properties live, with a formula), apply, effect, copy_look, mask (shapes, or a region of the backdrop: sky, water, trees...), particles (rain, snow, leaves, mist, sparks... by preset, into a region), living (recipes that bring a backdrop to life: water, foliage, clouds, rays, night, rain, storm, snow, mist), puppet (pins that bend a layer), scissor (cut lines that split the mesh so legs, arms and lips move apart), mask_puppet, corner_pin (perspective, skew), transition, reframe | three_d (layers in space), camera (add, aim, orbit / pan / dolly / truck / pedestal / roll / zoom), light (parallel, spot, point, ambient; shadows; glow, flare, beam, flicker, flames), atmosphere (the 3D scene's fog, weather, wet air, exposure, bloom, tone map and grade, by preset or fields), model (real 3D models: a shape or a human head to sculpt from, blended parts, a shape from mathematics (`math`: lattices, formula solids, fractals, parametric surfaces, knots, height fields, L-systems; skill 3d-assets-scenes part math shapes), or a glTF/OBJ/STL file; `wetness`, `emissive_strength` for wet and glowing surfaces), mesh (sculpt, subdivide, smooth, extrude, booleans, twist, bend, taper, warp, repeat, materials, textures on a model), parallax, shot (a camera framing subjects by name: "slow push in on Pip", sizes, angles, lenses, rigs), lighting (three-point, noir, golden hour, motivated by the backdrop…; lamps that glow or flicker and the air that goes with them), previs (stand-ins and a camera per shot from a shot list) | track (follow a point or a surface in the footage; layers, corner pins and masks follow it), stabilize (take out camera shake), cut_out (remove the background: people or a picked object), roto (a keyed path mask round an object), match_colour (shots match a reference shot's colour), smooth_motion (optical-flow in-betweens for slow motion and ramps) | shadow (a character's ground shadow, read from the backdrop's light), light_match (a character lit like the place it stands in), foreground (a rock, trunk or grass cut from the backdrop and laid over characters standing behind it) | film editing (guide topic editing): insert, overwrite, fit_to_fill, replace with marks (three- and four-point edits, picture and sound patched to lanes), ripple, trim_sides (asymmetric), extend, j_cut, l_cut, split_edit, lift, extract, sync_lock, target, sequence (new, duplicate, switch, nest), speed_ramp, string_out, selects, take (scene / take / circled), text_cut (cut by taking words out of the transcript) | lane, marker (name, colour, note, duration, on a clip), keyframe, raw. Read `guide` for fields and units. Returns only what changed.
